# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our application.

## No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal or usage data from your device. Your privacy is our highest priority.

## Data Storage and Syncing

All data generated or used within the app remains solely on your device or is securely synced through your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to your iCloud data, nor do we transmit any information to our servers or third parties.

## No Tracking or Sharing

We do not track user activity, nor do we share any data with advertisers, analytics providers, or other external entities.
